# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Accept feedback and differing technical opinions in good faith.
- Focus discussions on the project and its users.
- Protect private information and report security issues privately.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `support@wiro.ai`. Maintainers may remove content,
reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this
policy. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled as promptly and fairly
as possible.
